# Today

Home screen, called **Today** in the app's design: the day diary. A date strip, a compact card with the day's calories and macros, then the four meals with their entries and a + each. The code keeps its old names: view `DashboardView`, page `DashboardPage`.

`IconButton`, `Card`, `ListRow`, `BottomNav`, `DateStrip`, `EmptyState` and the snackbar here are components of the `Shields.UI` kit, not this app's; their stories are under Components on the [combined stories site](https://marty-shields.github.io/app-ui-kit/).

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Title "Calorie Tracker"; Settings gear (`IconButton`) on the right, opens Settings. No back arrow; back leaves the app. |
| Date strip | `DateStrip` under the bar: "Today, 19 Jul", the arrows move a day either way with no limit (future days too), and tapping the label returns to today. A future day looks like any other. The page opens on today, or on the day in the route's `date` (`/?date=yyyy-MM-dd`). |
| Calories | A compact kit `Card`: the kit's `ProgressRing` at its `Compact` size with "Eaten" and "Target" beside it, and the macros under them. The ring fills with the calories eaten of the target (full at or over it); its centre (the kit's `Figure` and `FigureUnit`, with an `AriaLabel`) shows the calories left with the caption "kcal left", and over the target the amount over, in the ring's over colour, with "kcal over". With no target set the ring shows calories eaten ("kcal eaten"), the card says "No target set" and **Set a target** opens Settings. The kit owns the centre number's width steps and its over colour. The ring's text alternative carries the numbers ("1,338 kcal left. Eaten 762 of 2,100 kcal target."). Whole kcal as the day summary rounds them. |
| Macros | The kit's `StackedBar` (label "Energy share") with protein, carbs and fat in the data series 1, 2 and 3, each segment sized by its calories (4 kcal per gram of protein and carbs, 9 per gram of fat) and labelled in grams ("0.#", e.g. "30.4 g"), inside the card under the ring. The screen-reader text follows the grams. Hidden while nothing is logged. |
| Empty day | `EmptyState` "Nothing logged today" ("Nothing logged this day" on any other day) with "Tap + on a meal to add your first food." shows under the card when no meal has an entry (the card has no macros then). |
| Meals fold | Every meal (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks) has a header button with `aria-expanded` and an arrow (up when open, down when folded); tapping it **folds** the meal to one line, "3 items · 348 kcal" ("1 item", or "Nothing logged" when empty), and tapping again opens it. The + stays a separate button. The defaults: on a **past or future day** every meal starts **folded**. On **today** Snacks and the meal whose window holds the time now start open and the rest fold (`MealWindows`: Breakfast 06:00 to 10:59, Lunch 11:00 to 14:59, Dinner 15:00 to 19:59, Snacks otherwise; `CurrentMeal` on `DashboardView` carries it). There is no "Now" chip and no start time. |
| Fold state | Taps are kept only in the view and never stored: leaving Today, or moving to another day, applies the defaults again. |
| After an add | Serving returns to `/?date=…` (`DashboardRoute.Url(date)`), a new Today with the defaults above, so an entry added to a meal that starts folded shows in its folded line ("2 items · 470 kcal") until the meal is opened. A usual food added from the Add sheet needs no navigation: `DashboardView` opens that meal itself when the add succeeds. |
| Meals | Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks, always in that order. Each is the kit's `SectionHeader` (title, the folded line as its hint once folded, and as its total the kcal once it is open and has entries; `data-testid` `meal-<meal>-title`, `-hint`, `-total` and `-toggle`) beside a filled + `IconButton` (`meal-<meal>-add`) and, when open, its entries in the order added. An open meal with no entries says "Nothing logged" (`meal-<meal>-none`) on any day. The + opens food search to add to that meal and the day on the strip (`/foods?meal=<Meal>&date=yyyy-MM-dd`). |
| Entries | `ListRow` with the food's title (brand and name, see Food search: "Weetabix Original"), the brand line (the first brand the title lacks) before the amount ("Ferrero · 15 g"), the amount ("40 g", "200 ml") or for an entry added by servings "1 serving · " and the label of the serving it was added by ("1 serving · ½ pizza (201.8 g)"; an entry saved before servings had their own table, or whose serving was deleted, shows "2 servings · 400 ml", the amount, instead), and the calories at the right. Tapping a row (`OnEdit`) opens Serving in edit mode for that entry, to change its amount or meal or remove it (see Serving). |
| Loading | The date strip stays, with a spinner "Loading…" where the day goes. |
| Load failed | The date strip stays, with "Couldn't load this day" and **Try again**. Also shown when an entry's food is missing. |
| Navigation bar | the kit's `BottomNav` with **Today** current, the raised **Add** button in the middle and **Trends** to its right; Foods and Scan are not in the bar. Add opens the `AddSheet` (through `AddSheetHost`, which Trends shares) (title "Add food", meal chips with the meal for the time of day selected, **Search foods** and **Scan barcode** and up to 3 usual foods of the chosen meal, each added in one tap at its last amount (the meal it went to is then open on Today, like after Serving), with the snackbar "<title> added to <Meal>" and Undo; a failed add keeps the sheet open with a message and a failed read hides the section), which sends the chosen meal and the day on the date strip on: `/foods?meal=<Meal>&date=yyyy-MM-dd` or `/scan?meal=<Meal>&date=yyyy-MM-dd`. The scrim, Android back and Escape close it without navigating, and focus returns to Add. See [`AddSheet`](./?path=/docs/calorie-tracker-components-layout-addsheet--docs). |
| Snackbar | A message posted by another screen, such as "Oat flakes added to Lunch" or, after Remove in Serving's edit mode, "Cheddar cheese removed", with **Undo** (which restores the entry), shows above the navigation bar (`<Snackbar />` in the `Status` slot). Nothing shows when none is posted. |

Dark theme: the same layout; switch the theme in the toolbar to see it.
