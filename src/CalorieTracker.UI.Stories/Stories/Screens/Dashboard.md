# Today

Home screen, called **Today** in the app's design: the day diary. A date strip, a compact card with the day's calories and macros, then the four meals with their entries and a + each. The code keeps its old names: view `DashboardView`, page `DashboardPage`.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Title "Calorie Tracker"; Settings gear (`IconButton`) on the right, opens Settings. No back arrow; back leaves the app. |
| Date strip | `DateStrip` under the bar: "Today, 19 Jul", the arrows move a day either way with no limit (future days too), and tapping the label returns to today. A future day looks like any other. The page opens on today, or on the day in the route's `date` (`/?date=yyyy-MM-dd`). |
| Calories | A compact kit `Card`: the `CalorieRing` at its `Compact` size (`--ring-size-compact` 6rem, `--ring-width-compact` stroke) with "Eaten" and "Target" beside it, and the macros under them (the ring's number steps down a size as it gets wider and its text stays clear of the band, see `CalorieRing`). Calories left; over the target the ring shows how many over, in the error tone. With no target set the ring shows calories eaten, the card says "No target set" and **Set a target** opens Settings. Whole kcal as the day summary rounds them. |
| Macros | The `EnergyShareBar` with protein, carbs and fat in grams, inside the card under the ring. Hidden while nothing is logged. |
| Empty day | `EmptyState` "Nothing logged today" ("Nothing logged this day" on any other day) with "Tap + on a meal to add your first food." shows under the card when no meal has an entry (the card has no macros then). |
| Meal windows | `MealWindows` in Core fixes each meal's time of day: Breakfast 05:00 to 11:00, Lunch 11:00 to 15:00, Dinner 17:00 to 21:00 (a window includes its start and not its end); Snacks has none. On **today** a meal is **open** during its window and shows a "Now" chip after its name and an up arrow, and is a button with `aria-expanded` like a folded one: it starts open and tapping the header folds it to the same one line ("3 items · 348 kcal" or "Nothing logged") with a down arrow; **before** its window it shows only its header with "from 11:00" (no rows, no "Nothing logged"); **after** its window it is **folded** to one line, "3 items · 348 kcal" ("1 item", or "Nothing logged" when empty), with a down arrow. A folded header is a button with `aria-expanded`: tapping it opens the meal (a past meal with no entries then says "Nothing logged") and tapping again folds it. The + stays a separate button. Snacks is always open and has no arrow. On **any other day** every meal is open, with no chip and no "from". |
| Reading the clock | The page reads the clock when the day loads and when the app resumes (`IAppStart`), not live, so a meal folds the next time the person comes back. `DashboardView` takes the time as `Now` (stories pass a fixed one); null reads every meal as open. Opened and folded meals are kept only in the view: leaving Today, or moving to another day, puts every meal back as its window says (a past meal folded, the Now meal open), and nothing is stored. |
| After an add | Serving returns to `/?date=…&open=<Meal>` (`DashboardRoute.Url(date, open)`): `OpenMeal` shows that meal open even when its window has passed, so the new entry and the snackbar line up. Any screen can send a person here the same way. A usual food added from the Add sheet needs no navigation: `DashboardView` opens that meal itself when the add succeeds (for the day on the strip, kept only until the day changes). |
| Meals | Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks, always in that order. Each is a `MealHeader` (name, "Now" chip or "from 11:00", total kcal once it has entries or the folded line, filled + `IconButton`) and, when open, its entries in the order added. A past meal opened with no entries says "Nothing logged", and so does any other open meal with no entries on a day that has some; on a day with nothing logged only the past meal does (a meal in its window, Snacks and another day's meals show just the header). The + opens food search to add to that meal and the day on the strip (`/foods?meal=<Meal>&date=yyyy-MM-dd`). |
| Entries | `ListRow` with the food's title (brand and name, see Food search: "Weetabix Original"), the brand line (the first brand the title lacks) before the amount ("Ferrero · 15 g"), the amount ("40 g", "200 ml") or for an entry added by servings "1 serving · " and the label of the serving it was added by ("1 serving · ½ pizza (201.8 g)"; an entry saved before servings had their own table, or whose serving was deleted, shows "2 servings · 400 ml", the amount, instead), and the calories at the right. Tapping a row (`OnEdit`) opens Serving in edit mode for that entry, to change its amount or meal or remove it (see Serving). |
| Loading | The date strip stays, with a spinner "Loading…" where the day goes. |
| Load failed | The date strip stays, with "Couldn't load this day" and **Try again**. Also shown when an entry's food is missing. |
| Navigation bar | the kit's `BottomNav` with **Today** current, the raised **Add** button in the middle and **Trends** to its right; Foods and Scan are not in the bar. Add opens the `AddSheet` (through `AddSheetHost`, which Trends shares) (title "Add food", meal chips with the meal for the time of day selected, **Search foods** and **Scan barcode** and up to 3 usual foods of the chosen meal, each added in one tap at its last amount (the meal it went to is then open on Today, even when its window has passed, like after Serving), with the snackbar "<title> added to <Meal>" and Undo; a failed add keeps the sheet open with a message and a failed read hides the section), which sends the chosen meal and the day on the date strip on: `/foods?meal=<Meal>&date=yyyy-MM-dd` or `/scan?meal=<Meal>&date=yyyy-MM-dd`. The scrim, Android back and Escape close it without navigating, and focus returns to Add. See [`AddSheet`](./?path=/docs/calorie-tracker-components-layout-addsheet--docs). |
| Snackbar | A message posted by another screen, such as "Oat flakes added to Lunch" or, after Remove in Serving's edit mode, "Cheddar cheese removed", with **Undo** (which restores the entry), shows above the navigation bar (`<Snackbar />` in the `Status` slot). Nothing shows when none is posted. |

Dark theme: the same layout; switch the theme in the toolbar to see it.
