# Serving

Choose an amount and a meal, then add the food to a day. Opened from a food (route `/serving/<food id>`; food search links to it, the Scan screen in a later change), or from a Dashboard entry in edit mode (see Edit mode). View: `ServingView`; page: `ServingPage`; route page: `Serving.razor` in the App.

Query parameters: `amount` and `servings` (without `entry`: the weight in g or ml, and the count of servings it was entered as, to open with instead of 1 serving or 100 g, such as what food search found logged last; servings mode only when the food has a serving; a weight that is not valid is ignored), `meal` (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snacks; a preset, such as from a meal's +), `date` (yyyy-MM-dd; today when missing or not a date), `return` (an in-app route such as `/foods`; Back and a saved entry go there), `back` (an in-app route for Back alone, so a meal's search can be returned to while a saved entry still goes to the Dashboard). Without them, Back goes to Foods and a saved entry to the Dashboard. After a saved entry a snackbar says "<food> added to <meal>": **View day** when `return` is set (the person goes back to where they came from), otherwise **Undo**, which removes the entry just added.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Back arrow; the food's title (brand and name, see Food search) as the heading. |
| Brand line | Muted, above the Per line: the brand line and the pack, joined by " · " ("Mars Wrigley · 51 g"). Left out when the food has neither. |
| Per line | Muted "Per 100 g · 258 kcal" for a food with a serving. A grams-only food adds macros: "Per 100 g · 416 kcal · P 25.4 · C 0.1 · F 34.9" ("Per 100 ml" for a liquid). |
| Enter as | Only when the food has a serving label and a weight above zero: the heading ENTER AS, a `SegmentedControl` Grams / Servings (Servings by default) and, in Servings mode, a card with the serving label. Foods without a serving show no switch, only grams (or ml). |
| Amount | Heading AMOUNT (Grams) or HOW MANY (Servings). A `NumberField` with the stepper: grams or ml in steps of 10, or a count of servings in steps of 1 (unit "serving"). Any positive number can be typed. In Servings mode a muted "= 202 g" shows the weight it comes to. In Grams mode a food with a serving shows **Use serving: <label>**, which switches to 1 serving. |
| Modes | Switching modes keeps the same weight: 150 g becomes 0.74 servings of a 201.8 g serving and back to exactly 150 g, until the number is edited. |
| Invalid amount | Zero, below zero, empty or not a number shows "Enter an amount above 0."; above 10,000 g or ml (`DiaryService.MaxAmount`) shows "Enter an amount of 10,000 g or less." The error shows in the error tone, the This adds card is hidden and Add is disabled. |
| Meal | Heading MEAL and `FilterChips` Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks. A preset meal is selected. With none preset the meal is suggested from the local time (Breakfast before 11:00, Lunch from 11:00, Dinner from 15:00, Snacks from 21:00) and "Suggested from the time: 12:40" shows under the chips. |
| This adds | A card: the kcal for the amount in large text (whole kcal) and a line. For a food with a serving the line is "202 g of Casa Roma Margherita pizza" (the title); otherwise "Protein 7.6 g · Carbs 0 g · Fat 10.5 g" for the amount. |
| Add | Bottom bar, primary: "Add to Lunch" (the selected meal). Saves the grams or ml; in Servings mode also the count of servings. While saving it shows "Adding…" busy. A failed save keeps the screen and shows "Couldn't add this food. Try again." above the button. |
| Edit mode | Tapping an entry on the Dashboard opens `/serving/<food id>?entry=<id>&meal=&date=&amount=&servings=` (the entry's own values; `ServingRoute.EditUrl`). The screen then differs from adding: a trash `IconButton` (aria-label "Remove entry", `serving-remove`) in the top bar; a muted "Lunch · 15 Nov" line (`serving-when`) in place of the Per line; no suggestion line; the card is headed THIS ENTRY; the bottom button is **Save** (`serving-save`, "Saving…" while busy) in place of Add. An entry added by servings opens in Servings mode with its count and its exact weight; one whose food no longer has a serving opens in grams. Save calls `DiaryService.UpdateAsync` (amount and meal; the day and food stay, and a moved entry keeps its place in the order added) and returns to the entry's day on the Dashboard; Back goes there too, whatever `return` or `back` say. The trash icon calls `RemoveAsync`, returns to the day and posts "<food> removed" with **Undo**, which calls `RestoreAsync` (same meal, day and place); with no Undo the entry stays removed. A failed save shows "Couldn't save this entry. Try again.", a failed remove "Couldn't remove this entry. Try again."; an entry removed meanwhile shows "This entry was already removed." and turns Save off (Remove then just returns to the day). A failed Undo says "Couldn't undo. Add the food again." and logs only the status (`UiEvent.RestoreFailed`). |
| Not shown | A spinner while the food loads; "Couldn't load this food" with Try again when the read fails; "Food not found" for an unknown id. |

**1 serving.** A food with no usable serving of its own but a pack of at most 350 g or ml (`FoodService.MaxPackServingAmount`, `PackageAmount` in the food's unit) is offered that pack as a serving labelled "1 serving" (`FoodService.PackServingLabel`), so a Mars bar (51 g) or a can of Coca Cola (330 ml) can be added in one tap. It never replaces a real stored serving, and a food with no pack, or a bigger one, gets no serving offer. The rule treats the pack as one portion only by its size: a 430 g cereal box or a multipack is over the limit and is not offered; a pack under it is, even when the box holds several portions. Choosing it logs the pack's weight with servings 1, and the diary row then reads "1 serving · 51 g", after the brand line when there is one (for Mars, "Mars Wrigley · 1 serving · 51 g"). The offer is made when `FoodService` reads the food and is not stored.

A food's serving label (at most 60 characters), serving weight (above 0, at most 10,000) and optional nutrients (fibre, sugars, saturates, salt: 0 to 100 g per 100 g or ml) are bounded by `FoodService` before the screen sees them; a serving out of range shows as no serving, a nutrient out of range as unknown.

Back: the `back` route, else the `return` route, else Foods.
