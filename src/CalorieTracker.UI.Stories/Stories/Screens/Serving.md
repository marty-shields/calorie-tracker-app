# Serving

Choose an amount and a meal, then add the food to a day. Opened from a food (route `/serving/<food id>`; food search and the Scan screen link to it), or from a Dashboard entry in edit mode (see Edit mode). View: `ServingView`; page: `ServingPage`; route page: `Serving.razor` in the App.

Query parameters: `amount`, `servings` and `serving` (without `entry`: the weight in g or ml, the count of servings it was entered as, and the id of the serving the count is in, to open with instead of 1 serving or 100 g, such as what food search found logged last; servings mode only when the food has a serving; a weight that is not valid is ignored; a `serving` the food does not have opens on the smallest), `meal` (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snacks; a preset, such as from a meal's +), `date` (yyyy-MM-dd; today when missing or not a date), `return` (an in-app route such as `/foods`; Back and a saved entry go there), `back` (an in-app route for Back alone, so a meal's search can be returned to while a saved entry still goes to the Dashboard). Without them, Back goes to Foods and a saved entry to the Dashboard. After a saved entry a snackbar says "<food> added to <meal>": **View day** when `return` is set (the person goes back to where they came from), otherwise **Undo**, which removes the entry just added.

**Servings.** A food has none, one or several servings, each a row of the `Serving` table (id, food id, label, amount in the food's unit, source, sort order). The source is *Product* (the serving Open Food Facts gives: at most one), *Pack* (the whole pack, from the package size; a food can have several, one for each pack size seen) or *User* (added by the person). A CoFID food has none until the person adds one. Rows are unique for a food, source and amount. They are listed smallest amount first (all are in the food's own unit); servings of one amount go product, then pack, then the person's own. The screen opens on the one a food was last logged with, else the first (the smallest).

- *One serving*: today's look, a card with the label and "= 202 g".
- *Two or more*: the card is replaced by a `SelectField` (`serving-select`) with each serving's label. Picking one keeps the count and uses that serving's amount for "= N g" (or ml) and for what is saved. The Add or Save saves the weight, the count and the serving's id; by weight saves no serving.
- *The pack serving.* Open Food Facts lookups and the background refresh write a Pack row for a package of at most 1000 g or ml (`FoodService.MaxPackServingAmount`), labelled by what it is and its size: "Can 330 ml" or "Bottle 1 L" when the source's wording of the size says can or bottle (or, for a drink, by shape: a common can size is a can, 1 L or more a bottle), else "Pack 330 ml" (rows stored by schema step 13 may still read "Whole pack (330 ml)"; schema step 15 gave every upgraded food a Pack row for each of its barcodes' packs up to 1000, so upgraded foods match refreshed ones). A food with several barcodes (see Scan) has a Pack row for each barcode's pack size; two of one amount are one row. When the pack is the food's only serving the screen words it "1 serving" (`FoodService.PackServingLabel`), so a Mars bar (51 g) or a can of Coca Cola (330 ml) can still be added in one tap and the diary row reads "1 serving · 51 g"; next to a product serving or the person's own it keeps the clear label, so it is not confused with them. A pack of the same amount as the product serving is left out as a repeat. The rule treats the pack as one portion only by its size: a multipack under 1000 g or ml is offered, and one over is not.
- *Add your own serving.* **Add your own serving** (`serving-own-add`) opens a form: a `TextField` for the name (at most 60 characters, `serving-own-label`) and a `NumberField` for the size in g or ml (above 0, at most 10,000, `serving-own-amount`), then **Save serving** (`serving-own-save`, off until both are valid) or **Cancel** (`serving-own-cancel`). Errors show under the field: "Use 60 characters or fewer.", "Enter an amount above 0.", "Enter an amount of 10,000 g or less."; a size the food already has as one of the person's own says "You already have a serving of that size." and a failed save "Couldn't save this serving. Try again." The saved serving becomes a User row, the screen opens on it counting 1, and Servings mode turns on when it is the food's first.
- *Delete.* While a User serving is chosen, **Delete serving: <label>** (`serving-own-delete`) deletes it; the screen opens on the smallest serving left, or in grams at the same weight when none is. Only the person's own servings can be deleted. **A diary entry added by a deleted serving keeps its amount, servings count and calories and loses only the link to it** (its `ServingId` is set to null), so its row then reads "2 servings · 400 ml" instead of naming the serving.
- *The diary row* names the serving the entry was added by ("2 servings · My mug"); an entry added by weight, one saved before servings had their own table and one whose serving was deleted show the amount.
- *Opening on a food.* With no serving named in the route, the screen opens on the smallest serving. Food search, a scan and an online search result all name the serving last used for a food logged before by servings (with its last amount and servings), so they open on that one while the food still has it, else on the smallest (the scanned pack is not preselected).
- *Opening on a logged food.* A food found in search that was logged before opens on the serving of its newest entry (by `serving`, else the smallest serving) with the same count.
- Servings are what a person typed or what a product says, so no log line carries a label or an amount, only ids and counts (`CoreEvent` 1011 to 1017).

A serving's label (at most 60 characters), its weight (above 0, at most 10,000) and a food's optional nutrients (fibre, sugars, saturates, salt: 0 to 100 g per 100 g or ml) are bounded by `FoodService` before the screen sees them; a serving out of range is left out, a nutrient out of range shown as unknown.

Back: the `back` route, else the `return` route, else Foods.
