# Serving

Choose an amount and a meal, then add the food to a day. Opened from a food (route `/serving/<food id>`; food search and the Scan screen link to it), or from a Today entry in edit mode (see Edit mode). View: `ServingView`; page: `ServingPage`; route page: `Serving.razor` in the App.

`IconButton`, `SegmentedControl`, `NumberField`, `TextField`, `Card`, `FilterChips`, `SelectField` and the snackbar here are components of the `Shields.UI` kit, not this app's; their stories are under Components on the [combined stories site](https://marty-shields.github.io/app-ui-kit/).

Query parameters: `amount`, `servings` and `serving` (without `entry`: the weight in g or ml, the count of servings it was entered as, and the id of the serving the count is in, to open with instead of the default amount (a count of one of the smallest serving, or 100 g), such as what food search found logged last; servings mode only when the food has a serving; a weight that is not valid is ignored; a `serving` the food does not have opens on the smallest), `meal` (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snacks; a preset, such as from a meal's +), `date` (yyyy-MM-dd; today when missing or not a date), `back` (an in-app route for Back, so a meal's search can be returned to; a saved entry always goes to Today). Without `back`, Back goes to food search (`/foods`, `ServingRoute.DefaultBack`) and a saved entry to Today. After a saved entry a snackbar says "<food> added to <meal>" with **Undo**, which removes the entry just added.

Both draw in the top bar's text colour (`--color-on-bar`). The page reads the state on load (`FavouriteService.IsFavouriteAsync`). A tap flips the icon at once, then writes: starring posts the snackbar "Added to favourites"; unstarring posts "Removed from favourites" with **Undo**, which stars the food again. The snackbar shows above the bottom bar (`<Snackbar />` in the `Status` slot). A failed write puts the star back and posts "Couldn't update favourites. Try again."; a failed read shows no star and the rest of the screen works. Starring never changes the amount, serving, meal or the Add or Save button. Opening the screen dismisses any snackbar carried over from another screen. Logs hold the food id and the status only (`UiEvent.FavouriteReadFailed`, `FavouriteWriteFailed`). |

Foods without a serving show no switch, only grams (or ml). |

| Edit mode | Tapping an entry on Today opens `/serving/<food id>?entry=<id>&meal=&date=&amount=&servings=&serving=` (the entry's own values; `ServingRoute.EditUrl`). Add the food again." and logs only the status (`UiEvent.RestoreFailed`). |

**Servings.** A food has none, one or several servings, each a row of the `Serving` table (id, food id, label, amount in the food's unit, source, sort order). The only servings are the ones the person adds (source *User*); nothing about servings is read from Open Food Facts, so a scanned or searched product starts with none and opens in grams or ml. Rows are unique for a food, source and amount. They are listed smallest amount first (all are in the food's own unit). The screen opens on the one a food was last logged with, else the first (the smallest), else in grams or ml.

- *One serving*: today's look, a card with the label and "= 202 g".
- *Two or more*: the card is replaced by a `SelectField` (`serving-select`) with each serving's label. Picking one keeps the count and uses that serving's amount for "= N g" (or ml) and for what is saved. The Add or Save saves the weight, the count and the serving's id; by weight saves no serving.
- *Add your own serving.* **Add your own serving** (`serving-own-add`) opens a form: a `TextField` for the name (at most 60 characters, `serving-own-label`) and a `NumberField` for the size in g or ml (above 0, at most 10,000, `serving-own-amount`), then **Save serving** (`serving-own-save`, off until both are valid) or **Cancel** (`serving-own-cancel`). Errors show under the field: "Use 60 characters or fewer.", "Enter an amount above 0.", "Enter an amount of 10,000 g or less."; a size the food already has as one of the person's own says "You already have a serving of that size." and a failed save "Couldn't save this serving. Try again." The saved serving becomes a User row, the screen opens on it counting 1, and Servings mode turns on when it is the food's first.
- *Delete.* While a User serving is chosen, **Delete serving: <label>** (`serving-own-delete`) deletes it; the screen opens on the smallest serving left, or in grams at the same weight when none is. **A diary entry added by a deleted serving keeps its amount, servings count and calories and loses only the link to it** (its `ServingId` is set to null), so its row then reads "2 servings · 400 ml" instead of naming the serving.
- *The diary row* names the serving the entry was added by ("2 servings · My mug"); an entry added by weight, one saved before servings had their own table and one whose serving was deleted show the amount.
- *Opening on a food.* With no serving named in the route, the screen opens on the smallest serving. Food search, a scan and an online search result all name the serving last used for a food logged before by servings (with its last amount and servings), so they open on that one while the food still has it, else on the person's smallest own serving, else in grams or ml.
- *Opening on a logged food.* A food found in search that was logged before opens on the serving of its newest entry (by `serving`, else the smallest serving) with the same count.
- Servings are what a person typed, so no log line carries a label or an amount, only ids and counts (`CoreEvent` 1011 to 1017).

A serving's label (at most 60 characters), its weight (above 0, at most 10,000) and a food's optional nutrients (fibre, sugars, saturates, salt: 0 to 100 g per 100 g or ml) are bounded by `FoodService` before the screen sees them; a serving out of range is left out, a nutrient out of range shown as unknown.

Back: the `back` route, else food search (`/foods`).
