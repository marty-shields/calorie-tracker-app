# Food search

Top-level screen, reached from the **Foods** item of the navigation bar (route `/foods`). View: `FoodSearchView`; page: `FoodSearchPage`. Two routes add a food to the diary and both end at the Serving screen: the Foods tab (a food's values gain **Add to diary**) and a meal's + (add mode, below).

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Title "Search foods". No back arrow, except while a food's values show; then the star too (`food-detail-favourite`, see Food selected). |
| Navigation bar | `BottomNav` with Foods current. A snackbar posted by the Serving screen shows above it (`<Snackbar />` in the `Status` slot). |
| Toolbar | Same layout as Exercises in the workout log app: the search box with the search `Icon` and "Search foods", pinned under the top bar with a line under it; the rows scroll beneath. Search box only, with no filter by kind of food. Hidden while a food's values or the load error show. Results update on every keystroke; a newer keystroke cancels the search still running. |
| Loading | On the first launch the food list is still being loaded in the background: the search box shows and a spinner with "Loading foods…" replaces the results. What is typed meanwhile is kept and searched once loading finishes. If loading fails the screen shows "Couldn't search foods" and Try again, which loads again. |
| Nothing typed | With nothing in the diary: "Search for a food" with the hint "Type a name, like oats or cheddar.". Otherwise the person's own foods, in groups under headings: first **Favourites** (every food starred as a favourite, A to Z by its title ignoring case and accents, ties by id; the group shows 5 and a "Show N more" row for the rest, which shows them all in place; it is the same on the Foods tab and in add mode), then in add mode **Usual for <meal>** (up to 5: the foods logged in that meal on the most days of the last 30), then **Recent** (up to 10: the foods of the newest entries, any meal, without any food already under Usual). The Foods tab has Favourites and Recent only. A favourite is never repeated under Usual or Recent, which are filled up to their limits from other foods. With favourites and an empty diary the Favourites group shows, not "Search for a food"; with no favourites the screen is as before. A group with no foods is left out. A favourite row looks like any other row (no star marker): it shows its last amount and calories when it was logged, else "N kcal / 100 g" (or ml), and a tap does what any row does. If reading the favourites fails the Favourites group is left out and the rest works. A food shows its last amount and the calories for it, like a diary row (below). If reading the diary fails the empty prompt shows. |
| Groups | While typing, up to four groups under headings, in this order: **Favourites** (only the favourites that match, in ranked order, the best 50 of them; the group shows 5 and "Show N more" like the others, and is left out when no favourite matches), **From your diary** (matching foods the person has logged, most logged first, ties in ranked order), **Common** (CoFID) and **Branded** (Open Food Facts foods stored from scans). A food is in one group only: a favourite is under Favourites and not again under From your diary, Common or Branded, and a logged food is under From your diary and not again under Common or Branded. A group with no matches is not shown. Each heading is an `h2` (a heading for a screen reader) with `data-testid` `food-group-<favourites, usual, recent, diary, common or branded>`. A group shows its first 5 foods and, when it has more, a "Show N more" row (N is how many foods the group holds beyond the 5 shown, which is what the row reveals) that shows the rest in place, in ranked order; it stays open until the text changes. A group holds only its best 50 foods when typed, so with more than 55 matches the row says "Show 45 more" and the open group lists 50. Before typing, Favourites holds all of them. Usual and Recent show all their foods. |
| Diary rows | A food the person has logged shows, in place of its energy per 100 g or ml, the last amount and the calories for it: "250 ml · 125 kcal", or "2 servings (80 g) · 320 kcal" when it was entered in servings, with the brand line and pack first when there are any (the same detail line as below). This holds under Usual, Recent and From your diary. With a meal (add mode) the last amount is the newest entry in that meal, or the newest in any meal when the food was never logged in that one. Other rows show "N kcal / 100 g" as below. |
| Rows | One `ListRow` per food: every typed word must match a word of the food, and the best matches come first: a main word (the name before its first bracket, plus the brand) equal to the typed word, then one it starts with, then a word inside the brackets (a word one letter off, typed words of 4+ letters only, counts only when no food matches the typed words by one of these: "bread" lists no "Broad beans"); a match in the last word of the name ranks higher, foods with fewer extra words rank higher, and for a single typed word a plain common food (such as Milk, average) ranks higher. Ties go A to Z. Its title (see Food titles below), and muted under it the brand line, the pack and then "N kcal / 100 g" (or "100 ml"), in that order, joined by " · " and skipping what is empty ("Ferrero · 400 g e · 539 kcal / 100 g" for Nutella), and a chevron. The food's type is not shown, and drinks, ingredients and foods share one list. Optional nutrients and servings out of range never reach the row or the values (the search bounds them like the Serving screen does). Tapping a row shows that food. |
| Search online | Once something is typed (and no food's values show), a full-width secondary `AppButton` "Search online" (`food-search-online`) sits under the last local group, and on the "No foods match" state it is the primary button with the same words and test id. It is always a button, never a list row. **Nothing is sent until it is tapped** (or the keyboard's search key is pressed): not on a keystroke, on opening the screen or in the background. The first time, a line under the button says "This sends what you typed to an online database." (`food-search-online-notice`); the first tap marks it as seen (a small setting, `OnlineSearchNoticeSeen`) and it never shows again. The tap sends the typed text and the filter for the phone's country (below), nothing else, once for each tap, except that a country with no match is repeated worldwide at once as part of the same tap: separate taps are at least 2 seconds apart (a tap that comes sooner waits for the rest, with the spinner showing) and a search finished once is kept in memory for the session, so the same text asks once. Typing again clears the results and brings the button back. |
| Searching | A spinner with "Searching online…" (`food-search-online-loading`) replaces the button. The local groups stay and stay usable. |
| Online results | A fourth group after Branded, headed "Online · <country>" (`food-group-online`; "worldwide" when the phone's language has no region, Open Food Facts has no country for it, or the country had nothing and the search was repeated once worldwide). Rows are like local ones (`ListRow`, title from `FoodDisplay`, brand line, pack, "N kcal / 100 g or ml", `food-online-<barcode>`) with a "NEW" aside when no stored food has the barcode. No photos. The list is cleaned before it shows: a hit with no barcode, name, unit or nutrition (kcal, protein, carbs and fat) is skipped (the unit is read from the pack text such as "330 ml", else from the categories: ml when one is beverages, grams when it has categories and none is beverages; a hit with no pack size and no category is skipped); hits with the same name (as the strict merge rule reads it), pack size and per-100 values collapse into the first; at most 20 are kept. The group shows 5 and a "Show N more" row (`food-group-online-more`) that shows the rest. The results are read-only: nothing is stored until a row is tapped. |
| Tapping a product | A spinner "Getting the product…" (`food-search-online-importing`) under the results, then the product is stored as a scan stores it (the barcode lookup, then the attach-or-create import, so the can and the 1 L bottle of one drink become one food with two pack servings) and the screen goes on as for any result: in add mode Serving opens on the serving last used for that food (with its last amount and servings; for that meal when it was logged there), when it was logged before by servings and still has that serving, else on the smallest serving (the pack of that barcode is not preselected); on the Foods tab the food's values show, and **Add to diary** opens Serving the same way. If the product can't be fetched the message below shows under the results with Try again, which fetches it again. If Open Food Facts has no usable product for the barcode, "That product can't be added" (`food-search-online-unavailable`) shows under the results with no Try again, and the other products stay tappable. While the product is being fetched the local rows do nothing, Enter on the keyboard sends nothing while foods are loading, and typing drops the fetch. |
| Busy, offline, failed | Under the local groups: "The online search is busy. Try again in a minute." (HTTP 429), "You are offline" (no response) or "Couldn't reach the online search" (anything else), each with Try again (`food-search-online-error`, `food-search-online-retry`), which repeats the search or the fetch. The local groups stay on screen and usable. A search that ran and found nothing usable says "Nothing found online" (`food-search-online-none`). |
| Country | The filter is the region of the phone's current language (`RegionInfo.CurrentRegion`, no permission), written inside the query as `countries_tags:"en:united-kingdom"`. Open Food Facts' own country list has a two-letter code for 252 of its 268 countries but writes the United Kingdom as UK where a phone says GB, so the app carries a generated table from device code to tag (`OffCountries.Generated.cs`, GB mapped explicitly). No region, or a code not in the table, searches worldwide. There is no "all countries" link and no setting. |
| No results | "No foods match" under the toolbar, then the primary **Search online** button (above). In add mode it is an `EmptyState` with the hint "Try another word." |
| Search failed | "Couldn't search foods" and Try again, which repeats the search. |
| Food selected | Tapping a result replaces the list with the `FoodValues` block: the food's title, its generic name when stored and different from the title, the brand line, the pack, "Per 100 g" (or "100 ml"), energy, protein, carbs and fat, plus fibre, sugars, saturates and salt when known. **Add to diary** (primary) and **Back to results** (secondary) are pinned in the screen's bottom bar, above the navigation bar, while the values scroll above them. **Add to diary** opens Serving with a meal suggested from the time of day, and with the last amount filled in when the food was logged before (see Add mode). **Back to results**, the back arrow and Android back return to the list with the search kept. The top bar has a star `IconButton` (`food-detail-favourite`) that stars the food as a favourite: `IconName.Star` (outlined, aria-label "Add to favourites") when it is not, `IconName.StarFilled` (aria-label "Remove from favourites") when it is, `aria-pressed` following the state, in the top bar's text colour. The page reads the state when the values open. A tap flips the icon at once and posts "Added to favourites", or "Removed from favourites" with **Undo** (stars it again), in the snackbar above the navigation bar. A failed write puts the star back and posts "Couldn't update favourites. Try again."; a failed read shows no star. There is no star on result rows or in add mode. |
| After Add (Foods tab) | Serving comes back to this search with the query restored (route `/foods?q=<text>`; an old `type` value in a link is ignored), the list showing, and the snackbar "<food> added to <meal>" with **View day**, which opens the Dashboard with the day in the link (`/?date=yyyy-MM-dd`; the Dashboard opens on that day). Back on Serving returns here the same way. |
| Add mode | Route `/foods?meal=<Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snacks>[&date=yyyy-MM-dd]`, which a meal's + opens. Title "Add to Lunch", a back arrow (and Android back) to the Dashboard, no navigation bar. Same search box and rows. Tapping a row opens Serving at once with that meal preset (no values step). A food logged before opens with its last amount filled in (and in servings mode, on the serving it was entered with, when it was entered by servings and the food still has a serving; else on its first serving); a food never logged opens as before (1 serving, or 100 g). The amount travels in the route (`amount`, and `servings` and `serving` with it); Serving reads it only when adding. After Add the person goes to the Dashboard with the snackbar "<food> added to <meal>" and **Undo**; Back on Serving returns to this search (`back` route). The meal's + on the Dashboard opens it, with the day on the date strip. |

**Food titles.** `FoodDisplay` in Core works out how a food is shown, and every screen uses it. The title is the name when the name already contains one of the food's brands (case, accents, spaces and hyphens ignored, so "Coca Cola" contains "Coca-Cola"), otherwise the first brand, a space and the name ("Weetabix Original"). The brand line is the first brand the title does not contain ("Ferrero" for Nutella, "Mars Wrigley" for Mars, none for Weetabix and Coca Cola). The pack is the stored `PackageLabel` ("430 gram", "400 g e"). A blank or missing brand leaves the name alone with no separator, and a CoFID food has no brand or pack, so its title is its name. The same title heads the diary row on the Dashboard, the Serving screen and the Scan result.

Back: from a food's values, the list; from the list, the Dashboard; in add mode, the Dashboard.
