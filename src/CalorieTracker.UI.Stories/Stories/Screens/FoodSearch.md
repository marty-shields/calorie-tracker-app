# Food search

Top-level screen, reached from the **Foods** item of the navigation bar (route `/foods`). View: `FoodSearchView`; page: `FoodSearchPage`. Two routes add a food to the diary and both end at the Serving screen: the Foods tab (a food's values gain **Add to diary**) and a meal's + (add mode, below).

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Title "Search foods". No back arrow, except while a food's values show. |
| Navigation bar | `BottomNav` with Foods current. A snackbar posted by the Serving screen shows above it (`<Snackbar />` in the `Status` slot). |
| Toolbar | Same layout as Exercises in the workout log app: the search box with the search `Icon` and "Search foods", pinned under the top bar with a line under it; the rows scroll beneath. Search box only, with no filter by kind of food. Hidden while a food's values or the load error show. Results update on every keystroke; a newer keystroke cancels the search still running. |
| Loading | On the first launch the food list is still being loaded in the background: the search box shows and a spinner with "Loading foods…" replaces the results. What is typed meanwhile is kept and searched once loading finishes. If loading fails the screen shows "Couldn't search foods" and Try again, which loads again. |
| Nothing typed | With nothing in the diary: "Search for a food" with the hint "Type a name, like oats or cheddar.". Otherwise the person's own foods, in groups under headings: in add mode **Usual for <meal>** (up to 5: the foods logged in that meal on the most days of the last 30), then **Recent** (up to 10: the foods of the newest entries, any meal, without any food already under Usual). The Foods tab has Recent only. A group with no foods is left out. A food shows its last amount and the calories for it, like a diary row (below). If reading the diary fails the empty prompt shows. |
| Groups | While typing, up to three groups under headings, in this order: **From your diary** (matching foods the person has logged, most logged first, ties in ranked order), **Common** (CoFID) and **Branded** (Open Food Facts foods stored from scans). A food is in one group only: a logged food is under From your diary and not again under Common or Branded. A group with no matches is not shown. Each heading is an `h2` (a heading for a screen reader) with `data-testid` `food-group-<usual, recent, diary, common or branded>`. A group shows its first 5 foods and, when it has more, a "Show N more" row (N is how many foods the group holds beyond the 5 shown, which is what the row reveals) that shows the rest in place, in ranked order; it stays open until the text changes. A group holds only its best 50 foods, so with more than 55 matches the row says "Show 45 more" and the open group lists 50. Usual and Recent show all their foods. |
| Diary rows | A food the person has logged shows, in place of its energy per 100 g or ml, the last amount and the calories for it: "250 ml · 125 kcal", or "2 servings (80 g) · 320 kcal" when it was entered in servings, with the brand first when there is one. This holds under Usual, Recent and From your diary. With a meal (add mode) the last amount is the newest entry in that meal, or the newest in any meal when the food was never logged in that one. Other rows show "N kcal / 100 g" as below. |
| Rows | One `ListRow` per food: every typed word must match a word of the food, and the best matches come first: a main word (the name before its first bracket, plus the brand) equal to the typed word, then one it starts with, then one letter off (typed words of 4+ letters only), then a word inside the brackets; a match in the last word of the name ranks higher, foods with fewer extra words rank higher, and for a single typed word a plain common food (such as Milk, average) ranks higher. Ties go A to Z. Its name, and muted under it the brand when there is one, then "N kcal / 100 g" (or "100 ml"), joined by " · ", and a chevron. The food's type is not shown, and drinks, ingredients and foods share one list. Optional nutrients and servings out of range never reach the row or the values (the search bounds them like the Serving screen does). Tapping a row shows that food. |
| No results | "No foods match" under the toolbar. In add mode it is an `EmptyState` with the hint "Try another word." |
| Search failed | "Couldn't search foods" and Try again, which repeats the search. |
| Food selected | Tapping a result replaces the list with the `FoodValues` block: the food's name, brand, "Per 100 g" (or "100 ml"), energy, protein, carbs and fat, plus fibre, sugars, saturates and salt when known. **Add to diary** (primary) and **Back to results** (secondary) are pinned in the screen's bottom bar, above the navigation bar, while the values scroll above them. **Add to diary** opens Serving with a meal suggested from the time of day, and with the last amount filled in when the food was logged before (see Add mode). **Back to results**, the back arrow and Android back return to the list with the search kept. |
| After Add (Foods tab) | Serving comes back to this search with the query restored (route `/foods?q=<text>`; an old `type` value in a link is ignored), the list showing, and the snackbar "<food> added to <meal>" with **View day**, which opens the Dashboard with the day in the link (`/?date=yyyy-MM-dd`; the Dashboard opens on that day). Back on Serving returns here the same way. |
| Add mode | Route `/foods?meal=<Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snacks>[&date=yyyy-MM-dd]`, which a meal's + opens. Title "Add to Lunch", a back arrow (and Android back) to the Dashboard, no navigation bar. Same search box and rows. Tapping a row opens Serving at once with that meal preset (no values step). A food logged before opens with its last amount filled in (and in servings mode when it was entered by servings and the food still has a serving); a food never logged opens as before (1 serving, or 100 g). The amount travels in the route (`amount`, and `servings` with it); Serving reads it only when adding. After Add the person goes to the Dashboard with the snackbar "<food> added to <meal>" and **Undo**; Back on Serving returns to this search (`back` route). The meal's + on the Dashboard opens it, with the day on the date strip. |

Back: from a food's values, the list; from the list, the Dashboard; in add mode, the Dashboard.
