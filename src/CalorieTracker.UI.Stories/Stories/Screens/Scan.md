# Scan

Scans a product's barcode and shows what it contains. View: `ScanView`; page: `ScanPage`. A found product goes to the Serving screen with **Add to diary**.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Title "Scan barcode", a back arrow, and Android back, to the Dashboard (on the day in the route's `date`). Scan is reached from the Add sheet's **Scan barcode**, at `/scan?meal=<Meal>&date=yyyy-MM-dd`; it has no navigation bar. |
| Content | **Opening camera**: a spinner labelled "Opening camera…" until the native camera covers the screen. **Closing camera**: a spinner labelled "Closing camera…" while the camera slides away after Cancel or Android back, so the screen never says "Opening camera…" on the way out. **Looking up**: a spinner. **Found**: the `FoodValues` block: the food's title as the heading (brand and name, see Food search), its generic name when stored and different from the title, the brand line and the pack (when known) and energy, protein, carbs and fat per 100 g, or per 100 ml for a liquid, plus fibre, sugars, saturates and salt when Open Food Facts has them. No serving line shows: servings are only the ones the person adds. **Not found**: an `EmptyState` with "Unable to find this product" and the hint "Try another barcode, or search by name.", with no reason. **Camera unavailable**: `LoadFailed` "Couldn't open the camera" with **Try again**, which restarts the scan (permission refused, or the scanner could not open). |
| Warning | Under the values when kcal differs from 4 × protein + 4 × carbs + 9 × fat by more than 20% (a placeholder to confirm). |
| Bottom bar | Found: a primary **Add to diary** above a secondary **Scan again**. Add to diary opens the Serving screen for the scanned food (its id; Serving reads the food and bounds its nutrients) with the route's meal and day (a scan without a meal suggests one from the time of day); Back from Serving goes to the Dashboard on that day (not back into the camera, which would lose the product), and so does a save. Add to diary is left out when the food could not be saved (no id); Search by name takes its place, as for not found. Not found: a primary **Search by name**, which opens food search for the route's meal and day, above **Scan again**. Camera unavailable has Try again in place of it. |

**One food for many barcodes.** A drink is sold in several sizes, each with its own barcode. A looked-up product is stored by `FoodImportService` (Core), which the scan only calls; it knows nothing about the camera. A barcode already stored shows its food, and the product is not stored again. For a new barcode the service looks for a stored Open Food Facts food that is the same product: the same unit, the same first brand after folding (not blank), the same name after folding with size words such as "330 ml", "1.5L" and "2 litres" taken out, and kcal, protein, carbs and fat, and fibre, sugars, saturates and salt, each within 2% of the larger value or 0.1, whichever is more (a nutrient known on one side only does not match). If there is one, the new barcode is added to that food with its pack; otherwise a new food is stored as before. Zero, Light, diet, sugar-free, flavour and "original taste" variants differ in name or nutrients, so they never merge, and a food is never merged across g and ml. The stored food keeps its name, brand and nutrients, and a later refresh of one barcode never writes another barcode's values to it. Each barcode keeps its pack (amount and label) on its own row, and no serving is stored for it. Add to diary opens the Serving screen on the serving last used for that food, with its last amount and servings, when it was logged before by servings and still has that serving, else on the person's smallest own serving, else in grams or ml (the scanned pack is not preselected). A product with no name, unit or needed nutrient is not stored, and the scan shows not found for it as it did before. Foods already stored as separate rows are not merged afterwards, and there is no screen to undo a merge.

The camera stays open until it sees a barcode of a valid length (8, 12 or 13 digits); other codes are ignored. That one barcode is normalised and looked up, and later detections are ignored: the scanner closes on the first accepted code, so no separate in-flight guard is needed. Not found, offline and rate limited all show the one not-found message, with no mention of the source or the reason. Cancelling the camera returns to the Dashboard. The native camera screen follows the app's chosen theme (System follows the phone): the page passes the saved choice to the scanner, which sets it as the app theme before opening the camera.
